NeuroTej AI

A Privacy-Preserving Edge Intelligence Platform for Real-Time Behavioral Analytics Across Educational, Examination, and Professional Assessment Environments.

Advantages

  • Low bandwidth consumption via edge processing.
  • Privacy preserved with zero raw video retention.
  • Real-time actionable insights and low latency.
  • Scalable ecosystem for diverse environments.

Architectures

1. Smart Classroom

Real-time computer vision at the edge delivers live attention scores, engagement trends, and learning effectiveness metrics directly to educators without exposing student data to the cloud.

2. ExamGuard

Ensures assessment integrity through gaze tracking, head pose estimation, and suspicious behavior detection, operating entirely on local hardware to protect candidate privacy.

3. Interview Analysis

Evaluates candidate communication, sentiment, and performance characteristics to estimate the best fit for job roles, providing objective, data-driven hiring intelligence.

Features

  • Live, custom alerts for administrators and proctors.
  • Multi-modal analytics combining vision, speech, and emotion.
  • Centralized, role-based decision-support dashboards.
  • Automated attendance and integrity tracking.

Problems & Solutions

The Problem: Heavy reliance on cloud infrastructure creates severe privacy risks, high bandwidth costs, and fragmented tools for monitoring virtual spaces.

The Solution: A unified Privacy-by-Design architecture where raw video remains at the edge. Only encrypted analytical outputs are transmitted, dramatically reducing risks and costs.
