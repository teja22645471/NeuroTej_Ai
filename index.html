The Vision

The Challenges

  • Unseen Disengagement: Organizations lack objective, real-time intelligence on participant engagement.
  • Cloud Vulnerabilities: Heavy reliance on cloud infrastructure creates severe privacy concerns and high bandwidth costs.
  • Biased Evaluations: Subjective and biased interview evaluation limits accurate candidate grading.
  • Fragmented Systems: Existing tools focus on isolated use cases rather than providing unified intelligence.

Our Solutions

  • Privacy First: Our framework processes raw visual data locally at the edge, ensuring zero video retention.
  • Real-Time Intelligence: Delivers instant, actionable analytical insights directly to decision-makers.
  • Objective Grading: Evaluates human behavior accurately without subjective bias.
  • Unified Architecture: A single scalable ecosystem powering classrooms, exams, and corporate interviews.

System Architectures

1. Smart Classroom

Delivers real-time engagement tracking, participation analysis, and attention scoring. Educators receive instant insights into learning effectiveness, empowering data-driven instruction without ever exposing student video to the cloud.

2. ExamGuard

Provides remote proctoring, precision eye tracking, suspicious activity detection, and comprehensive integrity scoring. Designed for secure assessments, it operates entirely on local hardware to protect candidate privacy.

3. Interview Analysis

Evaluates candidate communication, sentiment, and performance characteristics to estimate the best fit for job roles. By analyzing facial expression and vocal confidence, recruiters gain objective, unbiased grading.
