The Vision

The Challenge

Existing solutions rely heavily on cloud infrastructure, creating major vulnerabilities. Organizations struggle with:

  • Bandwidth Overload: Transmitting continuous raw video to the cloud is costly and slow.
  • Privacy Risks: Storing sensitive student and candidate visual data externally creates severe compliance risks.
  • Fragmented Systems: Organizations use disconnected, isolated tools rather than a unified intelligence system.

Our Solution

NeuroTej AI transforms live visual data into real-time engagement and behavioral intelligence through edge-first processing.

  • 100% Local Processing: Raw video remains at the edge and never leaves the room.
  • Zero Data Retention: Video is discarded instantly; only secure encrypted metadata is transmitted.
  • Real-Time Insights: Delivers instant, actionable metrics directly to a centralized decision dashboard.

System Architectures

1. Smart Classroom Analytics

Delivers instant engagement tracking and instructor effectiveness metrics directly to educators. By continuously monitoring the classroom environment locally, the system provides deep insights into learning dynamics and student participation, completely eliminating the need to expose sensitive student data to the cloud.

  • Privacy Preserved (Zero Cloud Video)
  • Real-Time Attention Scoring
  • Local Edge Processing
  • Instant Engagement Heatmaps
  • Automated Attendance Tracking
  • Instructor Effectiveness Metrics

2. Virtual Exam Intelligence

Ensures absolute assessment integrity through high-precision tracking and advanced behavioral risk analytics. The system operates autonomously on local hardware to monitor candidates in real-time, instantly identifying unauthorized materials or suspicious movements while fully protecting candidate privacy.

  • Privacy Preserved (Secure Local Analysis)
  • Precision Gaze Tracking
  • Automated Integrity Scoring
  • 360° Head Pose Estimation
  • Suspicious Object & Phone Detection
  • Unusual Audio & Whisper Alerts

3. Virtual Interview Intelligence

Provides objective, data-driven hiring intelligence by meticulously evaluating candidate communication, sentiment, and overall confidence. This multimodal analytical approach removes human bias from the recruitment process, ensuring candidates are graded fairly on their true behavioral and communicative competencies.

  • Privacy Preserved (Secure Processing)
  • Sentiment & Emotion Evaluation
  • Unbiased Candidate Grading
  • Advanced Communication Analytics
  • Vocal Confidence & Pacing
  • Behavioral & Micro-Expression Tracking
